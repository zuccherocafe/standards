# Skills

These are the skills zucchero✿café projects' agents use. Each folder holds one `SKILL.md`. A project's development shell links them for tools that load skills, and its `AGENTS.md` points other tools at the ones each role needs.

## Ours

`setup-repository`, `change-write`, `change-review`, and `change-land` are zucchero✿café's own, under the repository's [CC-BY-4.0](../LICENSE).

## Copied from pstack

The other skills come from [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) and keep its MIT licence, below. What we changed:

- unslop: loads on its own, with a description saying it applies to all prose.
- technical-writing: unchanged.
- principle-encode-lessons-in-structure, principle-fix-root-causes, principle-test-behavior-not-implementation: load on their own.
- principle-attack-the-premise: loads on its own; links to pstack skills not copied here, and its closing paragraph on one of them, removed.
- principle-explain-the-number: loads on its own; its closing comparison with a pstack skill not copied here removed.
- benchmark-checklist: loads on its own; references to pstack playbooks not copied here removed.
- ketchup: names its voice directly instead of a pstack skill; a Cursor-only tool is described as a chart or diagram.
